[S4 Gas Model] The gas model
[S4 Gas Model] TRK-02
[S4 Gas Model] The protocol does not compress for you. Every byte you upload is a byte you pay for forever.
[S4 Gas Model] TapeCode writes your site path-for-path into an on-chain contract. No gzip, brotli or WebP. The bill is fixed:
[S4 Gas Model] Constant
[S4 Gas Model] Value
[S4 Gas Model] Meaning
[S4 Gas Model] CHUNK_BYTES
[S4 Gas Model] 24,000
[S4 Gas Model] Chunks of 24,000 bytes — one chunk, one transaction
[S4 Gas Model] perChunk
[S4 Gas Model] 270,000 gas
[S4 Gas Model] Fixed cost per chunk (the tx, the directory slot, the event)
[S4 Gas Model] perByte
[S4 Gas Model] 235 gas
[S4 Gas Model] Every single byte, forever
[S4 Gas Model] 地板价
[S4 Gas Model] 0.05 gwei (BSC)
[S4 Gas Model] gasPrice = max(floor, min(live, 20 × floor))
[S4 Gas Model] Single-file limit
[S4 Gas Model] 8 MiB
[S4 Gas Model] Larger files are skipped silently; the UI says nothing
[S4 Gas Model] The formula
[S4 Gas Model] gas ≈ chunks × 270,000 + bytes ×
[S4 Gas Model] 235
[S4 Gas Model] Reference price
[S4 Gas Model] 1 MB 纯数据 ≈ 0.0129 BNB ≈ $9.60
[S4 Gas Model] BSC floor 0.05 gwei · BNB $747 · baseline 2026-10
[S4 Gas Model] Prices and congestion change the amount; this recalculates as you edit the assumptions.
[S4 Gas Model] Many small files cost more than one big file
[S4 Gas Model] Chunks round up per file. Ten 3 KB files = ten chunks = 2.7M gas of per-chunk overhead for 30 KB.
[S4 Gas Model] Change one file, pay for one file
[S4 Gas Model] Same path + sha256 is skipped. Changed files re-upload in full — 240 KB → 60 KB saves on every revision.
[S4 Gas Model] Don't publish junk
[S4 Gas Model] README.md, .map and design exports are billed by the byte. Hidden files (.shots/, .DS_Store) are skipped entirely.

[S8 Iteration] Long-term cost
[S8 Iteration] TRK-06
[S8 Iteration] Iteration cost doesn't scale with site size — it scales with the files you touch.
[S8 Iteration] Same path + same sha256 is skipped. Anything that changed is re-uploaded in full.
[S8 Iteration] Implication: an 800 KB site changing only a 20 KB JS pays 20 KB per deploy. Big assets become free after one change — one-time cost; code is the recurring cost.
[S8 Iteration] Strategy / 6 rounds
[S8 Iteration] 6-round total
[S8 Iteration] Notes
[S8 Iteration] A · No compression
[S8 Iteration] $10.39
[S8 Iteration] Source shipped raw; changes billed at uncompressed bytes
[S8 Iteration] B · Shrink + incremental
[S8 Iteration] BEST
[S8 Iteration] $5.03
[S8 Iteration] Always ship the shrunk build; the protocol skips unchanged files

[S8 Iteration] Strategy C  $10.43  >  $10.39  Strategy A — switching shrink settings mid-way costs more than never shrinking.
[S8 Iteration] 01
[S8 Iteration] Ship the shrunk build from round one
[S8 Iteration] Switching midway re-uploads everything — possibly worse than never shrinking.
[S8 Iteration] 02
[S8 Iteration] Keep shrink settings stable
[S8 Iteration] Changing psnrTarget or the aggressive preset re-uploads affected files; run a delta bill first.
[S8 Iteration] 03
[S8 Iteration] Shrink the files you keep changing
[S8 Iteration] Big images are a down payment; index.html and the main JS are the real recurring cost.
[S8 Iteration] Two free lunches
[S8 Iteration] Comment-only edits cost nothing (comments are dropped → same bytes → skipped). A new unoptimized image can also be free (shrinks to the old bytes).

[S9 Limits] Limits
[S9 Limits] TRK-07
[S9 Limits] What we don't do is on this page, too.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] No font subsetting
[S9 Limits] Fonts are 176.5 KB — 44% of the shrunk total. Subsetting needs re-cut glyphs and a codepoint-coverage check, or text turns to tofu. The tool just reports: ~88 KB more.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] We never send a transaction
[S9 Limits] It only touches the file system — no transactions, no signing. Publishing still goes through TapeCode.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] .well-known/ can't be uploaded
[S9 Limits] A path segment starting with . counts as hidden and is skipped — no domain verification or ACME here.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] Files over 8 MiB fail silently
[S9 Limits] The protocol skips them silently. We flag them; the publishing page won't.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] Changing shrink settings re-uploads the whole site
[S9 Limits] Change a setting and nearly every file's bytes change — treated as brand-new content.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] Dead-file detection is conservative
[S9 Limits] It reads static references only; runtime names fall back to basename / dir-prefix matching — better to miss one than delete a live file.
[S9 Limits] SKIPPED
[S9 Limits] PSNR is not a model of the human eye
[S9 Limits] PSNR isn't a model of the eye; 42 dB is where these images looked identical. On another site, raise it and re-compare.
[S9 Limits] A shrink tool that hides its limits isn't one you should hand your site to.
